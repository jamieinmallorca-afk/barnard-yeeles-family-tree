✅ Yeeles Branch — Going Further Back Updated Aug 2026

The 1871 England census and Kevin Yeeles's MyHeritage tree (Dickenson Web Site) together confirm the full line:

  • Isaac Yeeles (b.1807, Marshfield, Gloucestershire; d.1851) — Henry's father.
  • Hannah Hibbert (b.1803; d.1884) — Isaac's wife, Henry's mother. Maiden name confirmed as Hibbert.
  • Henry Yeeles (b.1830, Marshfield) — son of Isaac & Hannah. Married Jane Dickenson (b.1836). All 7 children: Elizabeth (1859), George Thomas★ (1865), Mary Selina (1867), Mary Pelina (1867), Eliza (1869), Sarah Jane (1871), Edith Rose (1879).
  • Jane Dickenson (b.1836, Gloucestershire) — Henry's wife. The "Dickenson" in Kevin's tree title refers to her maiden name.
  • George Thomas Yeeles (b.1865, Marshfield) — confirmed in Kevin's tree.
  • Annie Maria (b.1872, St Catherine, Somerset) — George Thomas's wife. Handwritten records give her maiden name as Toghill; she appears in the 1901 census at Wellow, Somerset.

Dickenson ancestry traced to 1728. Kevin's tree goes back three more generations on Jane's side: Thomas Dickenson (1800–1878, Gloucestershire) m. Mary Shewrance — they had 11 children including Jane. Thomas was the son of William Dickenson (1766–1844) m. Mary Rice (1763–1852); and William in turn was the son of William Dickenson (1728–1811) m. Dorothy Goslett (b.1722). These four Dickenson generations are now shown in the tree above.

How far back does the Yeeles family go? Isaac Yeeles (b.1807) is the oldest recorded in Kevin's tree. The Marshfield parish registers (St Mary's) show Yeeles baptisms as far back as 1640 — Robert and John Yeeles, sons of William Yeeles. The family has been rooted in Marshfield for nearly 400 years.

Note: Rockett family. Kevin's tree also contains a Rockett family branch (Maurice Rockett b.1814 + Susan Rockett b.1812 and their 5 children including Emily Rockett). This appears as an isolated subtree with no clear connection to the Yeeles/Dickenson line in the data examined. Further research needed.

Still to research: Annie Maria Toghill's parents and Elsie Lavina Dixon Weichel's parents. Isaac Yeeles's parents (pre-1807) would require Marshfield parish records.

✅ Barnard Branch — What We Now Know New

Research via FreeBMD, RAF biographical records, and FamilySearch confirmed the Barnard paternal line back four generations:

  • Air Cdre. John Owen Barnard CBE, OBE (21 May 1917 – 12 May 2003) — Richard's father, your paternal grandfather. Born Mutford district, Suffolk. Distinguished RAF career reaching Air Commodore.
  • → Son of Ernest Thomas Barnard & Mabel May Rackham, married June 1900 in Mutford, Suffolk (GRO Vol 4a, Page 1988 · FreeBMD).
  • → Ernest was son of Mark Barnard (1842–1912) & Mary Elizabeth Booth (b.1844), married 1871 in Mutford, Suffolk (FamilySearch 9CJX-77M).

JO & Elizabeth's children: Nick Barnard, Richard Barnard (your father), and Judith Elizabeth Ann Barnard — Richard and Judy are twins, both born 27 March 1952. Judy married Nigel B Brueton and lives in Cobham, Surrey. Their children — your cousins — are Hannah, James and Alexander Brueton.

Still to find: Elizabeth Barnard's maiden name (JO's wife, Richard's mother). It would appear on Richard's 1952 birth certificate.
