✅ Yeeles Branch — Going Further Back Updated Aug 2026

The 1871 England census and Kevin Yeeles's MyHeritage tree (Dickenson Web Site) confirm the full line:

  • Isaac Yeeles (b.1807, Marshfield; d.1851) — Henry's father.
  • Hannah Hibbert (b.1803; d.1884) — Isaac's wife, Henry's mother.
  • Henry Yeeles (b.1830, Marshfield) — married Jane Dickenson (b.1836). All 7 children: Elizabeth (1859), George Thomas★ (1865), Mary Selina (1867), Mary Pelina (1867), Eliza (1869), Sarah Jane (1871), Edith Rose (1879).
  • Jane Dickenson (b.1836) — Henry's wife. The "Dickenson" in Kevin's tree title refers to her maiden name.
  • Annie Maria (b.1872, St Catherine, Somerset) — George Thomas's wife. Handwritten records give maiden name as Toghill; appears in 1901 census at Wellow, Somerset.

Dickenson ancestry traced to 1728. Kevin's tree goes back three more generations on Jane's side: Thomas Dickenson (1800–1878) m. Mary Shewrance — father of Jane. Thomas was son of William Dickenson (1766–1844) m. Mary Rice (1763–1852); William was son of William Dickenson (1728–1811) m. Dorothy Goslett (b.1722). These are all shown in the right ancestry column above.

Still to research: Annie Maria Toghill's parents and Elsie Lavina Dixon Weichel's parents. Isaac Yeeles's parents (pre-1807) would require Marshfield parish records.

Research via FreeBMD, RAF biographical records, and FamilySearch confirmed the Barnard paternal line back four generations:

  • Air Cdre. John Owen Barnard CBE, OBE (21 May 1917 – 12 May 2003) — Richard's father, your paternal grandfather. Born Mutford district, Suffolk.
  • → Son of Ernest Thomas Barnard & Mabel May Rackham, married June 1900 in Mutford, Suffolk (GRO Vol 4a, Page 1988 · FreeBMD).
  • → Ernest was son of Mark Barnard (1842–1912) & Mary Elizabeth Booth (b.1844), married 1871 in Mutford (FamilySearch 9CJX-77M).
